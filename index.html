Once upon a time in a town of Luvid. He doesn't want to walk and rest. But the thing is he wanna ran always and ever. His name is Drei, he is the one who runs so fast and nimble. Till the day he want to make his own playground. And want to play with the kids in Luvid town. Suddenly his mother came going home in Luvid town. And caught him playing in the playground. Her name is Mudra. Mudra is the strict mother of Drei. She want to discipline him very much. But for being tolerated of Drei's self of still running and with no rest. Drei disrespect his mother. Mother Mudra said, he you Drey! I told you that if you respect the parent. The parent will respect him too. But Drei denied it and still start playing around with tiredness. As the story ends for almost 1 month passed. And Drei still don't respect himself and still play with kids around the town of Luvid. Then at night all of the kids of Luvid town is already left. Because it's dangerous for them to play so much at night. Then when kids everywhere sleeps in night. But Drei denied it for almost 1 month. Suddenly the hermit came. His name is Racky, the hermit one who punishes the kid like Drei to turn him into a rabbit. By lack of sleep and still running and playing around the town of Luvid. And when Drei caught the anonymous one. Drei said, Hey who are you!. Hermit Racky said, I am the hermit who disciplines the children. I can see it though you know, that your mother that time disciplines you. But you still don't make rest in just 1 month. And still you there are running over and over again. Hmm, I think I will give you a sanction enough. I will turn you into a rabbit so can't ever sleep ever than never. Drei said, No please. Hermit Racky said, too late because of your tolerations or just accepting who you are. And not thinking respect for your mother. I'm so sorry you never discipline your mother. Then I never discipline you too. Drei said, Noooo!!!... Hermit Racky already cast a spell and turn Drei being normal kid. He turns into a "Legend Of The Rabbit". Where he can never ever sleep anymore. But in the next 1 day when normal kids of Drei plays in the playground. Drei already can't play with the kids in the Luvid Town. But he can only play with the kids in the playground by start running. And never been sleep anymore. As the lesson of not obeying the rules with his mother. The name Drei is not his name anymore. His already the rabbit always running and not sleeping. Then Drei the animal rabbit. Is played by friends or kids by him. Moral Lesson: Don't abuse too much running and playing. Be clever to spent some sleep to make an energy. Have time to not been tired. And still ready to face everything. By choosing respect to elderly. You can do whatever you want. You can't do it if you make thinking stupid. That is not healthy to play or run and even without resting for respect. So be discipline to care a healthy self.
